# Privacy Policy (Dotto)

[日本語](../)

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Dotto ("the App") respects the privacy of children and their parents.
This policy explains what information the App handles.

## 1. We do not collect any information
The App **does not collect any information about you, and nothing leaves your device**.

- The App has no servers of its own and does not connect to the internet.
- The App contains no advertising, analytics, tracking, or crash reporting.
- There are no accounts or sign-ins, and you are never asked for a name, age, or email address.
- There are no in-app purchases.

## 2. Information stored on your device
- **Artwork**: the pixel art you paint (which square is which color), the date you painted it, and whether it matches the example. This is used to show your album and to let you continue painting.

This information never leaves your device. Deleting the App deletes all of it.

## 3. Device permissions
- **Photos (add only)**: used only when you choose "Save to Photos" on the "Done!" screen and answer the parent check, to save your picture to the Photos app. The App never reads your photos.

You can turn this permission off at any time in the Settings app.

## 4. Parent check
Before any action that reaches outside the App, such as saving to the Photos app, a parent is asked to answer a math question.

## 5. Sharing with third parties
Because the App collects no information, nothing is shared with third parties.

## 6. Changes
If this policy changes, we will post the update on this page.
