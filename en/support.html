FAQ

How do I play?

Pick an example on the first screen, choose a color at the bottom, then tap or drag your finger over the squares to paint them. When your picture matches the example, you get “Perfect!”. The number under each color shows how many squares of that color are left. To erase a square, tap it again with the same color. Pick a different color and tap to repaint it. “Undo” (the arrow button) takes back the last step.

What are “Hints”?

When Hints are on, the squares you haven’t painted yet show the example’s colors faintly. Use them when a picture feels hard. Finish without Hints to get a gold star, or with Hints to get a silver star. “Check” makes the squares painted in a different color from the example blink.

The squares are too small to paint

For “Very hard” and “Master” pictures, spread two fingers apart to zoom in, and move two fingers to look around. Painting always uses one finger. The button at the bottom right shows the whole picture again.

Is my picture saved?

Yes. It is saved automatically, so you can continue the next time you open it. To paint the same example again, tap “New” after “Done!”. You can see all your pictures in the “Album” on the first screen.

How do I delete a picture from the album?

Tap “Tidy up” at the top right of the album, choose the picture, then tap “Erase”. Deleted pictures cannot be restored.

How do I save to the Photos app?

Tap “Done!”, then “Save to Photos”. A math question for parents appears; enter the answer to save the picture. The first time, please allow adding to Photos.

The app closes while my child is playing

Swiping from the edge of the screen can return to the Home Screen. Guided Access (Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access) keeps your iPhone or iPad in one app.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No. The App does not collect or send any information. See the Privacy Policy for details.
